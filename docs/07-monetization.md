# Monetization — Ethical, Cosmetic-Only

## 1. The rule

> **Power is never sold.** Every item that touches the simulation — operators,
> gadgets, fork charges, map access — is earned in play. The store sells
> *identity*: how your crew looks, sounds, and celebrates.

This is not just ethics; it's design coherence. The game's fantasy is
*outsmarting a fair system*. Selling power would poison the determinism
promise ("the echo never lies") with "…unless someone paid."

## 2. What is sold

### 2.1 Battle Pass (seasonal, 10 weeks)

- **Free track**: operator skins pieces, emblems, echo-rig color themes,
  in-game currency, a cosmetic vault finisher.
- **Premium track (~$9.99)**: full operator skin sets, drone skins, heist
  intro animations, profile banners, bonus currency that *only* buys
  cosmetics.
- Pass progression comes from playing heists (successful runs, clean-run
  bonuses, co-op plays) — not grind-walls. A casual player (≈4h/week)
  finishes the track; the pass rewards playing, not living in the game.
- No FOMO mechanics on *power*: anything gameplay-relevant from a past season
  remains earnable through the standard progression.

### 2.2 Direct cosmetics

- Operator skin sets (rarity tiers: standard → signature).
- Echo visualizers: how your ghost futures render (color, particle style,
  trail shapes) — purely visual, never changes prediction *information*.
- Vault finishers, emotes, victory poses, drone companions (visual only).
- Facility decoration for the crew hideout (social space).

### 2.3 Founder Pack (one-time, launch window)

- Unique signature skin set + badge that **never** returns (honest
  exclusivity: marked "Founder — Season 0").
- 30-day cosmetic-only subscription trial, launch-week Discord role,
  digital artbook + soundtrack.
- Priced as a thank-you (~$29.99), contents strictly cosmetic, clearly
  itemized before purchase.

### 2.4 Subscription ("Kestrel Club", ~$4.99/month)

- Monthly cosmetic drop (one skin piece + one echo visualizer).
- Extra hideout slots, replay export (video), name color.
- **Never**: XP boosts, currency discounts on power, early gadget access,
  matchmaking priority.

## 3. Explicit non-monetization list

| Not sold | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Operators (new crew members) | Roster power must be identical for all players |
| Gadgets / EMP / fork charges | Direct sim power |
| Map access or early missions | Content gating by payment splits the community |
| Loot boxes with gameplay items | No paid randomness at all; cosmetics sold direct with visible contents |
| Currency → power conversion | Ledger-enforced: premium currency buys cosmetics only (see `06-security-anticheat.md` §5) |

## 4. Store design principles

- **See everything before buying**: 3D preview, video, exact contents. No
  bundled mystery.
- **Pricing transparency**: one premium currency, published conversion rate,
  no dark-pattern dual-currency confusion.
- **Refunds**: standard platform refunds + goodwill window for accidental
  purchases.
- **Kid-safety**: no countdown-pressure pop-ups in-game; store is a separate
  destination, not an interstitial.

## 5. Revenue mix (target, year 1)

| Stream | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Battle Pass | 45% |
| Direct cosmetics | 30% |
| Founder Pack (launch window) | 15% |
| Subscription | 10% |

ARPU target driven by cosmetics *desirability* (skin quality, rarity
honesty), not by friction. LTV lever: seasons every 10 weeks with fresh
cosmetic themes aligned to campaign arcs (see `03-story-bible.md`).
